International students in Riga

Latvia had 11.9 thousand international students in the 2025/26 academic year, 16% of all 76.4 thousand students in higher education (CSP counts students whose previous education was abroad). The largest groups came from India (28.7%), Uzbekistan (11.1%), Sweden (8.6%). Most study in Riga, so student rooms go fast before September and February.

University dorms and their prices

Dorms are the cheapest option and usually include utilities, but places are limited and often go to first-year and exchange students first. Monthly prices per person:

The University of Latvia asks for a minimum stay of two months. RSU’s prices are for its own students; guests from other schools pay more. Riga Technical University runs its own housing in Ķīpsala, so check its prices directly.

Renting a flat as a student

A private studio costs €336 a month on average across Riga, before utilities. Teika, Imanta and Purvciems are the cheapest areas we track, from about €300.

Sharing is often cheaper than living alone. A 3-room flat (a living room and two bedrooms) averages €530 in Purvciems and €490 in Imanta, so two students pay about €265 and €245 each before utilities. Check that the lease names both of you, so each tenant can declare the address.

  • Near RTU in Ķīpsala: few flats and higher rents. Āgenskalns, on the same side of the river, is cheaper and has trams into the centre.
  • Lower rents, longer trips: Purvciems and Imanta are the cheapest, with buses, trolleybuses or the suburban train into town. Teika is a good middle ground, with public transport straight down Brīvības gatve.

A student budget

  • Rent: from about €105 for a shared dorm room or €220 for a single, or €336 for an average private studio plus utilities.
  • Transport: €12 a month with a student e-ticket, against €30 at full price.
  • Phone and internet: a mobile plan with 10 GB or more costs about €17.46 a month (Numbeo).
  • Food: see the everyday prices in our cost of living guide.

Never pay a deposit for a flat you haven’t seen in person or on a live video call. Fake listings aimed at students arriving from abroad are common before each semester.

Quick answers

How much is a student dorm in Riga?

From about €105 a month for a bed in a shared room to €525 for a single room in a newer hostel. A single room at the University of Latvia’s Valdemāra 161 dorm starts at €220.

How much does a studio flat cost in Riga for students?

The average asking rent for a studio (1-room flat) in Riga was €336 a month in 2026, before utilities. The cheapest areas start at about €300.

Do students get cheaper public transport in Riga?

Yes. Students studying in Riga pay €12 a month on a personalised e-ticket, against €30 at full price.

How many international students are there in Latvia?

11.9 thousand in 2025/26, about 16% of all higher-education students (CSP).
