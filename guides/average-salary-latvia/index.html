Average salary in Latvia in 2026

The average full-time gross wage in Latvia was €1,886 a month in the second quarter of 2026, or €1,401 after tax, according to the Official Statistics Portal (CSP). Riga pays the most of any region, with an average of €2,068 gross.

The average is pulled up by high earners. The median, the pay of the worker in the middle, was €1,527 gross and €1,168 net. The minimum wage rose to €780 a month in 2026, from €740.

How salary tax works in 2026

On a regular payslip three things come off your gross pay:

  • Social insurance: 10.5% paid by you. Your employer pays another 23.59% on top of your salary.
  • Non-taxable minimum: the first €550 a month (after social insurance) is tax-free, plus €250 for each dependant. This is applied at your main job, where your electronic salary tax booklet is held.
  • Income tax: 25.5% on the rest. Annual income above €105,300 is taxed at 33%, and income above €200,000 pays an extra 3%; both are settled in the annual tax return.

How far a salary goes on rent

A common rule of thumb is to keep rent under about 30% of take-home pay. On the average Latvian net wage of €1,401, an average 1-bedroom flat in Riga (€489) takes 35%. Only Purvciems and Imanta come in around 30%; Ķīpsala takes 62%.

Riga salaries are higher than the national average. Using the 2026 tax rules, the average Riga gross wage of €2,068 works out at about €1,519 take-home, which brings the average 1-bedroom flat down to 32%.

Checking a job offer in Riga

  • Ask whether the figure is gross or net. Latvian offers and job ads usually quote gross monthly pay.
  • Work out the take-home pay with the calculator above, then budget rent at about a third of it.
  • Add the living costs. Numbeo puts one person’s monthly costs without rent at about €869. Our cost of living guide breaks it down.
  • Ask about relocation help. Some employers pay the first month’s rent or the agency fee.

Quick answers

What is the average salary in Latvia in 2026?

€1,886 a month gross and €1,401 net for full-time work in Q2 2026 (CSP). The median was €1,527 gross.

What is the average salary in Riga?

€2,068 a month gross in Q2 2026, the highest of any region. That is about €1,519 after tax under the 2026 rules.

What is the minimum wage in Latvia in 2026?

€780 a month gross, up from €740 in 2025.

What is the income tax rate in Latvia?

25.5% on annual income up to €105,300 and 33% above it, plus 3% on income over €200,000. Employees also pay 10.5% social insurance, and the first €550 a month is tax-free.
