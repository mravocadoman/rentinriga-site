What else you’ll pay

Deposit

Usually one month’s rent. Latvia’s Residential Tenancy Law caps it at two months.

Agency fee

Commonly 50% of one month’s rent on agency listings. Flats rented straight from a private owner often have no fee.

Utilities

Heating, water, waste, the building fee and electricity come on top. An un-insulated 45 m² flat can cost around €116–142 a month to heat in December–January, against about €46–57 in an insulated building (Rīgas siltums example, excl. VAT).

Quick answers

What is the average rent in Riga in 2026?

Across Riga listings from March to October 2026, the average asking rent was €336 a month for a 1-room flat, €489 for a 2-room flat and €709 for a 3-room flat, excluding utilities.

Which Riga neighbourhood is the cheapest to rent in?

Of the areas we track, Purvciems had the lowest average 2-room asking rent at €420 a month. Ķīpsala was the highest at €870.

Are utilities included in Riga rents?

Usually not. Heating, water, waste, the building management fee and electricity are paid on top, and heating costs most in winter, especially in un-insulated buildings.
