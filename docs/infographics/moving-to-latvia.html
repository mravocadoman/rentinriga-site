Moving to Latvia: the checklist

  1. Check which route applies to you

    EU, EEA and Swiss citizens need no permit; everyone else needs a residence permit.

  2. Line up your work, study or business

    It decides your permit and your budget.

  3. Find a home

    Your lease is the basis for registering your address.

  4. EU citizens: get a registration certificate

    In person at PMLP if you stay longer than 3 months.

  5. Get a personal code

    Comes with a residence permit, or ask PMLP.

  6. Declare your place of residence

    Within 1 month. Free online at latvija.gov.lv.

  7. Register with a family doctor

    Your way into state-funded care, if you’re covered.

  8. Sort out getting around

    Monthly ticket €30; students pay €12.

Official rules: pmlp.gov.lv, vmnvd.gov.lv. Check the latest before you apply.
