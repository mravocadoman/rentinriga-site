What to expect

What it’s like

Pre-war and Soviet-era blocks among parks, with shops on Brīvības gatve. Several large office complexes nearby.

Getting around

Public transport along Brīvības gatve runs straight to the centre.

Good for

Offices in the Teika / VEF area; good value with an easy commute.

What moving in costs here

For an average 2-room flat in Teika at €505 a month, expect about €1,262 due when you sign. That covers the first month’s rent, a one-month deposit (refundable) and an agency fee of half a month, if one is charged. Utilities come on top, and heating is the big one in winter.
