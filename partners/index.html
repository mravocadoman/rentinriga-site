What you get

Every request arrives complete

  1. The full brief

    Move-in date, length of stay, who’s moving (adults, children, pets), max rent, rooms, furnished or not, preferred areas and must-haves.

  2. Contact and consent

    Name, e-mail, phone or WhatsApp and the preferred channel. Every tenant has explicitly agreed to be contacted by partner agencies.

  3. Context that helps you prioritise

    Whether an employer is paying or relocating them, and any notes about the office location, schools or parking.

Our standards

What we ask of partners

People new to Riga are easy targets for bad deals, so every partner agrees to:

Reply within one working day

Newcomers often decide quickly. A fast first reply is the most important thing you can do.

Speak English

At least one agent on each request has to handle viewings and contracts in English.

Show before taking money

No deposit or reservation fee before an in-person viewing or a live video walk-through of the actual flat.

Be upfront about fees

State your commission and the expected utility costs before the viewing, and use a written contract.
