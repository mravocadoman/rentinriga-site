Privacy notice

This notice explains what we do with the details you send through rentinrigalatvia.lv, who we share them with and what your rights are.

Who we are

Riga Flat Match (rentinrigalatvia.lv) is run by Rihards Zeila, Riga, Latvia, who is the data controller. Contact: rihards.zeila@gmail.com, +371 26 673 384.

What we collect

  • Your rental request: move-in date, length of stay, number of adults and children, pets, budget, rooms, furnishing, preferred areas, must-haves, employer (if you give it) and any notes.
  • Contact details: name, e-mail, phone or WhatsApp number (optional) and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • Agency applications: agency name, website, contact person, e-mail, phone, portfolio size and areas covered.
  • Technical context: the page and language version you sent the form from, how you reached us (for example, the campaign tag in a link or the referring website), and the time you agreed to this notice.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To pass your request to up to three partner rental agencies so they can contact you with offers. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you give with the checkbox on the form.
  • To reply to you and handle your request or application, including one automatic e-mail confirming a rental request. The legal basis is taking steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To prevent spam and abuse, and to see which channels bring requests. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who receives it

  • Partner rental agencies. We send your request to up to three of them, only with your consent. Once they receive it, each agency is responsible for your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Service providers that process data for us: n8n (form processing and storage), Google (Gmail, for the e-mail we receive), Telegram (a short alert with your name and contact details) and SiteGround (website hosting).
  • Assets on the website: when you scroll to the map, the site loads the map library from Cloudflare’s cdnjs and the map images from OpenStreetMap. These providers receive your IP address when your browser fetches those files. Fonts are served from our own server.
  • Visit statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies and doesn’t follow individual visitors across sites; Cloudflare receives your IP address, the page and the referring website when the page loads. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which pages people use (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We never sell your data or add you to a mailing list.

Cookies and storage on your device

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies. While you fill in the request form, your answers are saved in your own browser’s storage so a reload doesn’t lose them, and they’re cleared when you send the form. We also keep a note of how you arrived for the duration of your visit. None of this is shared until you press send.

How long we keep it

We keep rental requests and agency applications for 12 months after we receive them, then delete them. We delete them sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by e-mailing rihards.zeila@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent stops any further sharing, but it doesn’t undo what was shared before. You also have the right to complain to Latvia’s Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).
