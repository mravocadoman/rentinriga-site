# Partner outreach kit

Everything needed to sign the first rental agencies. Nothing in this folder is deployed; the deploy uploads only the site files it lists.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `targets.md` | Riga rental agencies to contact, with contacts from their own websites, and a status column to track replies |
| `email-en.md`, `email-lv.md` | First e-mail. Use Latvian for Latvian agencies, English for the rest. Replace `{first name}` / `{vārds}` and `{agency}` / `{aģentūra}`. |
| `followup-en.md`, `followup-lv.md` | One follow-up after 4–5 working days with no reply. Then stop. |
| `rent-in-riga-partner-offer.pdf` | One-page offer to attach. The source is `partner-offer.html`; re-render with Chrome's "print to PDF" (A4, no margins) after editing. |
| `partner-agreement.md` | Short agreement for agencies that say yes. **It's a draft: have it checked and decide which entity signs before the first signature.** |

## Offer in one line

First 10 leads free, then €20 per lead (plus VAT if applicable), at most three agencies per request, no monthly fee, stop any time.

## How to run it (weeks 1–2 of the plan)

1. Send 5 e-mails a day from your own address, not a mailing tool, starting with the agencies marked as the best fit in `targets.md`.
2. Log each send and reply in `targets.md`.
3. On a call, ask three questions:
   - What do you pay for a lead or listing today?
   - What share of serious enquiries turn into a signed lease?
   - Which areas and price ranges do you want?
4. Goal: 3 signed partners. If fewer than 2 say yes, rethink the model before spending on ads.
5. Once a partner signs, add them to the routing. Leads keep going to you by e-mail until automatic routing is built (planned once 2 or more partners have signed).

The Latvian e-mails went through the latvian-writing review pass. The remaining reviewer notes are listed in the session summary for the owner to decide.
