Riga Flat Match

Partner offer for rental agencies

Tenant requests from people relocating to Riga

Foreigners moving to Riga for work fill in one request on rentinrigalatvia.lv. We pass it to no more than three agencies that cover their areas and budget, and you take it from there.

How it works

  1. A tenant sends a request in English or Russian, with explicit consent to be contacted by partner agencies.
  2. We check it and send it by e-mail to up to three partners whose areas and price range fit.
  3. You contact the tenant, arrange viewings in person or by video, and sign the lease on your usual terms.

Terms

First 10 leads free

Then €20 per lead (plus VAT if applicable), invoiced monthly.

  • No monthly fee, no long contract, stop any time.
  • Each request goes to three agencies at most.
  • Your usual commission and lease terms stay yours.

Why these leads

  • People actively relocating, often with a fixed start date.
  • Complete briefs, so you can shortlist flats before the first call.
  • Employer-backed requests are flagged.
  • Neighbourhood guides, a rent-prices page and a cost calculator are built to bring renters in through search.
