Stay safe

How to avoid rental scams in Riga

Scammers target newcomers who can’t view a flat in person. These four rules protect you, whoever you rent through.

  • Never pay before a viewingSee the flat in person or on a live video call where the agent walks through it, and check that the keys work.
  • Sign a written contractIt should list the rent, the deposit, what’s included, the notice period and the inventory. Ask for an English copy.
  • Check who owns itA real agent or owner can show proof of ownership or a signed agreement with the owner. The owner of any flat can be checked in the Land Register (zemesgramata.lv).
  • Pay by bank transferPay to a named account and get a receipt. Avoid cash handed to strangers, crypto and money transfer services.

FAQ

Renting in Riga: the short answers

What does Riga Flat Match cost me?

Nothing. Our partner agencies pay for the service. An agency may charge you its own commission if you rent through it, and partners agree to tell you about it before you commit to anything.

How much is the agency fee in Riga?

There’s no fixed rate. On agency listings it’s commonly 50% of one month’s rent, paid by the tenant. Flats rented straight from a private owner often have no fee. Ask about the fee before you book a viewing.

How big is the security deposit?

Usually one month’s rent. Latvian tenancy law caps it at two months’ rent. The landlord has to return any unused part when you move out, unless your contract says otherwise. Deductions are for unpaid bills or damage.

Are utilities included in the rent?

Usually not. Expect separate bills for heating, water, waste, the building management fee and electricity, plus internet. Heating is the big one. It runs through the cold months, and an un-insulated building can cost two to three times as much to heat as an insulated one. Ask for last winter’s bills.

Can I rent for just one to three months?

Yes, but there’s less choice, because most landlords prefer longer leases. Serviced apartments are the other option. Choose “1–3 months” in your request and we’ll send it to agencies that handle short lets.

Can I arrange everything before I arrive?

Often, yes. Many agents do live video viewings and send the contract for e-signing. Never pay a deposit for a flat nobody has shown you on a live video call or in person.

Do I need to register my address?

Yes, if you live here on a residence permit or an EU registration certificate. You declare your place of residence within one month of moving in, and you can do it online at latvija.gov.lv. Your lease is the legal basis for the address. EU citizens staying longer than three months must also get a registration certificate from the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. Check the current rules at pmlp.gov.lv.

What should the lease include?

Under Latvia’s Residential Tenancy Law (2021), a lease must be in writing and fixed-term. It should state the rent, how and when the rent can change, the deposit, who pays which bills, and an inventory of the furniture. Tenants can usually end the lease with one month’s written notice. Read the full law at likumi.lv.

Is Riga Flat Match a landlord or an agency?

Neither. We’re a free matching service. We don’t own or list flats. We send your request to independent rental agencies, and you sign the lease with them or with the owner.
